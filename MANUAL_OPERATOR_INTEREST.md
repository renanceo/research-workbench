# Gate 1 Manual Operator: Expression of Interest

**Status:** open for one independent operator

The project is seeking one researcher to serve as the manual paper-positioning
operator for a bounded evaluation. This is a procedural baseline role, not a
request to agree with the product or model outputs. Formal evaluation cases are
not public and are not shared during recruitment.

## Candidate profile

A suitable candidate should:

- have real experience submitting, reviewing, editing, or evaluating research
  papers;
- be able to assess interdisciplinary theoretical or computational research;
- preferably know at least one adjacent area such as complex systems,
  theoretical biology, statistical physics, or computational biology;
- have had no role in developing this evaluation's prompts, private profiles,
  thresholds, or calibration materials; and
- agree not to judge any case that they operate.

No professorial title is required. The project is recruiting one qualified
person, not creating an operator pool.

## Independence and confidentiality

Before qualification, the candidate must attest that they:

- have not seen model-system smoke outputs, judge answers, sealed commitments,
  or formal evaluation cases;
- will not seek access to another system's output while operating a case;
- will use only the tools and official-source retrieval access declared in the
  private qualification packet; and
- will not disclose manuscripts or private evaluation materials.

The public repository does not contain the positioning protocol, prompts,
private venue or community profiles, scoring rules, thresholds, calibration
records, benchmark cases, or judge materials.

## Qualification process

1. The candidate submits the minimal eligibility statement below.
2. The benchmark custodian checks experience and conflicts against rules fixed
   before any formal case is shown.
3. One eligible candidate receives the private frozen procedure and a
   permanently development-excluded qualification fixture.
4. The candidate independently produces the required structured report within
   the predeclared time, tool, retrieval, and clarification limits.
5. Qualification checks procedural compliance, completion time, and output
   validity only. A different venue choice, community judgment, or risk order
   from the product or a model is not a qualification failure.
6. A machine-verifiable qualification record is retained in the private
   benchmark repository. The public repository receives no manuscript or
   private-protocol content.

The qualification fixture and its output cannot be used to train the operator
toward product conclusions. Formal evaluation remains blocked until the
operator is qualified.

## Minimal eligibility statement

Prospective operators may express interest through the repository's public
issue or discussion channel when available, using only:

```text
operator_handle: <stable pseudonym>
research_experience: <short summary>
submission_or_review_experience: <short summary>
relevant_fields: <short list>
independence_attestation: I did not participate in prompt, private-profile,
  threshold, or calibration development and will not judge cases I operate.
availability: <brief availability window>
```

Do not post legal identity documents, confidential manuscripts, unpublished
reviews, employer records, or other sensitive evidence in a public issue.
Additional verification, if needed, is handled privately by the benchmark
custodian.
